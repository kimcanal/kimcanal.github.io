👋 About Me

I've always liked taking things apart — PCs, phones, tablets, game consoles — and putting them back together working. As a kid I flashed custom ROMs with Odin and adb, learning mostly from XDA threads and YouTube; these days I still do things like flashing global ROMs onto China-market tablets. At Sogang University, where I study Computer Science & Engineering, I finally learned what was happening underneath: C, operating systems (Pintos), and systems programming.

I don't have one framework I'd call “mine”; what I do have is a habit of understanding systems from the bottom up. I build full-stack projects with AI coding agents and use my CS fundamentals to check their work — integration and concurrency tests, CI gates, and evaluating my game's AI against an exact solver. That's how a multiplayer dice game, a café's ordering site and a FastAPI service went from idea to deployment in weeks rather than months.

I also served in the Republic of Korea Air Force as a Special Intelligence specialist. I'm looking for a backend or systems role where I can learn production engineering properly.
